About Our Society

Transforming Tech Education in Prayagraj & Beyond

IT HUNT in Holagarh, Prayagraj is a top-tier Computer Technology Training Institute servicing students locally and across Uttar Pradesh. Established with a vision to make digital literacy accessible to all, we specialize in rapidly evolving fields like Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, Web Development, and Financial Accounting.

Under the guidance of renowned faculty, our institute provides live project classes, practical lab environments, and official examination prep for government recognized diplomas.
